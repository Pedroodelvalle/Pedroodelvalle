## Why Conty exists

AI is accelerating content, but trust still belongs to real people. Conty is the infrastructure that lets creators recommend products they truly use while brands get transparent performance at scale. Think of it as the "Uber" layer for spontaneous, story-driven commerce.

- Anyone can earn by sharing a genuine product story.
- Brands tap into authentic UGC without the agency overhead.
- The marketplace optimizes for trust, not vanity metrics.
