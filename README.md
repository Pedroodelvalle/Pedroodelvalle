## 🚀 About Me

22 years old • São Paulo, Brazil • Dropout • Builder

In a world increasingly dominated by AI, **authenticity becomes the last frontier**. 

At Conty, we're building a platform where anyone can earn money promoting products they genuinely love — naturally, spontaneously, just by sharing their story. Think of it as the **"Uber" of the creator economy**: connecting brands with authentic voices at scale.
